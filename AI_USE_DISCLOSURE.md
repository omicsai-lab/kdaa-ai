# AI-use disclosure and author responsibility

Generative-AI and coding-agent assistance was used **materially** across conceptual scaffolding, software architecture, code generation, test generation, documentation, figure preparation, and quality-control review for this research alpha. It was not used as an autonomous author, source of empirical evidence, or substitute for accountable scientific judgment.

The named author and maintainers remain responsible for:

- the focal problem and theory–artifact mapping;
- acceptance, rejection, and revision of generated material;
- source and citation verification;
- software review, testing, security, and release decisions;
- all claims made in papers, talks, repositories, and downstream applications; and
- journal-, conference-, employer-, and institutional-policy compliance.

The repository is designed to enforce the same epistemic distinction internally: machine-generated text is an **asset hypothesis or opportunity suggestion**, not validated knowledge. Active claims require trace-level provenance and bounded scope; high-impact use requires accountable human review and, where relevant, outcome evidence.

Any manuscript based on this repository should include venue-specific disclosure of material AI assistance. The exact disclosure should reflect the final human contribution, validation performed, and the policy in force at submission time.
